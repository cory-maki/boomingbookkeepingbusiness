The Mission

Making Bookkeeping Accessible to Everyone

Bill Von Fumetti founded Booming Bookkeeping Business in 2018 with a clear purpose: prove that anyone — regardless of background or experience — can build a profitable, location-independent bookkeeping business.

His flagship Keyboard Rich framework has become a trusted pathway for career-changers, stay-at-home parents, and aspiring entrepreneurs. The system walks students step-by-step through learning QuickBooks Online, setting up their business, landing clients, and delivering professional services with confidence.

With over 8,000 students served, strong reviews on Trustpilot and the BBB, and a featured speaking role at QuickBooks Connect — the industry's premier conference — Bill has established himself as one of the most trusted voices in virtual bookkeeping education.
