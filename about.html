The Beginning

From Idea to a Movement

Bill Von Fumetti launched Booming Bookkeeping Business in 2018 after recognizing a gap in the market: small businesses constantly need reliable bookkeeping, yet most people have no idea the profession is accessible without a degree or years of experience. Bill set out to change that.

What started as a simple training program quickly grew into a full educational ecosystem — with a book, a free 5-day challenge, a mentorship program, and a thriving community of students across the country. Today, Booming Bookkeeping Business is one of the most recognized names in the online bookkeeping education space.

The Keyboard Rich Framework

A Proven Pathway to Bookkeeping Success

At the core of Bill's work is the Keyboard Rich framework — a structured approach to building a virtual bookkeeping business from scratch. It covers everything from learning QuickBooks Online to setting up your business structure, acquiring your first clients, and delivering services with professionalism and confidence.

The framework is designed for beginners. Students consistently report that the training breaks down every step in simple, actionable terms — even those with zero background in accounting or finance. The emphasis is on real-world execution, not just theory.

Student Success

Over 8,000 Students and Growing

Booming Bookkeeping Business has helped over 8,000 students take meaningful steps toward financial freedom. Graduates include stay-at-home parents returning to the workforce, corporate employees seeking a flexible side income, and full-time entrepreneurs who've replaced their 9-to-5 entirely.

Reviews on Trustpilot, the BBB, and the company's own platform consistently highlight the clarity of the curriculum, the quality of the mentorship, and the supportive community environment. Many students describe the program as the most practical and motivating business training they've encountered.

Industry Recognition

Speaking at QuickBooks Connect

Bill has earned recognition far beyond his own platform. He has spoken at QuickBooks Connect — the premier annual conference for bookkeeping professionals — sharing his insights on how to build and grow a virtual bookkeeping business in today's economy.

His work has been covered by third-party review publications and educational blogs, and Booming Bookkeeping Business maintains an active, verified presence on the Better Business Bureau. Bill is also the author of the book Keyboard Rich, which lays out his core methodology for readers who want to explore the opportunity at their own pace.

The Bigger Picture

Building Economic Opportunity

Bill's mission has always extended beyond teaching bookkeeping. He believes that practical skills, combined with the right support and guidance, can fundamentally change a person's financial trajectory. Bookkeeping is not a trend — it's a stable, in-demand service that every small business needs, making it one of the most reliable foundations for an independent income.

As the future of work continues to shift toward remote and flexible arrangements, Booming Bookkeeping Business remains focused on equipping people with the skills, confidence, and roadmap they need to build sustainable, independent income from anywhere.
